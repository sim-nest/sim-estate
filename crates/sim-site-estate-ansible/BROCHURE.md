# sim-site-estate-ansible

In one line: Sealed Ansible provider site for SIM estate operations.

## What it gives you

Discover and operate an estate through sanitized identities and durable typed evidence while keeping private provider material behind a narrow site boundary. Raw inventory, task arguments, facts, diffs, exceptions, and human logs stay in caller-owned private artifacts rather than portable records. Fixed operation bindings and bounded process requests prevent product callers from selecting arbitrary programs, paths, environment variables, or shell syntax. Dispatch uncertainty and controller loss remain explicit provider outcomes.

## Why you will be glad

- Private infrastructure details do not leak into public records or user projections.
- Fixed bindings make the effect surface small enough to audit.
- Typed evidence lets the model site and physical provider share conformance tests.

## Where it fits

This is a concrete provider site behind the portable estate contract. It translates reviewed operations into bounded process requests. The process port, durable book, guarded organ, public projection, and approval decision remain separately owned.
