# sim-lib-estate-serve

In one line: Read-only estate Dir projection and capability-shaped organ calls.

## What it gives you

Inspect providers, inventory, plans, review state, runs, events, quarantine, and rebuilt summaries through one coherent directory surface. Rows are projections of durable records, so readers do not gain hidden mutation authority. Effects remain explicit typed calls into the guarded organ, with capability checks and exact arguments at the boundary. Terminal, visual, SDK, and automation surfaces can therefore share the same product model without each growing a private command protocol.

## Why you will be glad

- One projection keeps every user surface aligned with the same durable facts.
- Read access stays separate from effect authority.
- Typed calls make automation reviewable without exposing shell or path control.

## Where it fits

This crate is the product-facing adapter above estate core, book, and organ libraries. It owns projection and call shapes. Rendering, transport, provider behavior, storage engines, and approval policy remain outside its boundary.
