# sim-lib-estate

In one line: Guarded plan, apply, and reconcile organ for SIM estates.

## What it gives you

Preview and review a content-bound change, then apply it exactly once through a capability-checked organ. Durable history records intent before dispatch and captures verification, cleanup, and reconciliation afterward. Target exclusion prevents conflicting work from proceeding concurrently. Ambiguous dispatch, controller loss, and unverifiable outcomes enter quarantine rather than being reported as success. The organ composes provider contracts, approvals, the durable book, and policy without baking host-specific behavior into its core.

## Why you will be glad

- Effects require an exact reviewed plan rather than mutable ambient input.
- Unknown outcomes remain visible and recoverable through explicit reconciliation.
- Provider-neutral orchestration keeps policy consistent across model and physical sites.

## Where it fits

This is the effect-owning application layer above portable records and durable history. It coordinates providers but does not implement command execution, inventory parsing, storage, terminal rendering, or the human review decision.
