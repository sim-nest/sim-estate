# sim-estate-acceptance

In one line: Bounded physical-controller acceptance driver for SIM estate operations.

## What it gives you

Exercise the integrated estate stack against an explicitly selected controller and private project root while emitting only sanitized, digest-bound acceptance evidence. The driver composes discovery, compiled operations, bounded process execution, durable history, read-only projections, model refusals, and offline verification. Fixed program and artifact registries keep arbitrary shell, path, and environment authority outside the test surface. Read-only capture proves provider wiring without making an estate change or claiming a private operational outcome.

## Why you will be glad

- One bounded specimen checks that the portable contracts compose on a real controller.
- Sanitized evidence can be reviewed without copying private infrastructure facts.
- Model and physical checks share exact categories and digest fields.

## Where it fits

This non-publishable package is terminal acceptance evidence, not a product library. It drives existing estate crates through their public boundaries. The human decision, private project contents, provider credentials, and production scheduling remain external and explicitly selected.
